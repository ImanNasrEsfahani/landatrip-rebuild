# Phase 5: isolated checkout contract and activation decisions

## Verified design boundary

The live booking entry point remains blocked with HTTP 409. The synthetic API is under `/api/__e2e/checkout`. Its routes are registered only in local or testing application environments. Every handler also checks **all** of the following: `BOOKING_TEST_CHECKOUT_ENABLED=true`, `BOOKING_TEST_GATEWAY=fake`, local or testing application environment, MySQL at the E2E container host `mysql` using account `landatrip_e2e`, configured and connected database name exactly `landatrip_e2e`, a matching randomly generated fixture marker inserted by `E2eSeeder`, and a positive configured hold duration. The handler guard remains effective even if a locally generated route cache is reused elsewhere. No synthetic route calls the payment facade, Stripe, mail, SMS, or a travel provider. A passing fake callback cannot activate live checkout.

The versioned server contract is `synthetic-cad-v1`. `group_price_items` identifies mandatory versus optional items, category, version, currency, and a nullable `DECIMAL(12,2)` `amount_exact`. The fixture has one mandatory **CAD 42.35** adult item and one optional **CAD 7.20** adult extra. It also retains a USD item to prove mixed currency rejection. These values are synthetic arithmetic fixtures and have no commercial meaning. Only the `adult` category is supported by this fixture; all other categories are rejected. One mandatory item is required for each supported category. Optional quantities are explicit and may not exceed participant count. Empty, duplicate, invalid, cross-version, and cross-currency selections are rejected.

Amounts are parsed from decimal strings into integer CAD cents. Multiplication and summation use integers. The fixture uses an explicit **0% tax** and **zero discount**; neither is a live policy. The contract defines tax as basis points on subtotal after discount, with half-up rounding to the nearest cent. The fake gateway amount is an integer count of CAD cents. No exchange or implicit currency conversion occurs.

The original `group_price_items.amount`, `invoice_details.price`, and invoice monetary columns remain `DOUBLE` for historical compatibility. Synthetic invoices also store nullable exact decimal subtotal, discount, tax, and total fields, decimal detail line totals, and the immutable quote snapshot. Their legacy DOUBLE fields are compatibility mirrors; quote arithmetic, payment minor units, and callback checks use the exact fields and snapshot. Historical invoices are never assigned a synthetic marker or version. Rollback preserves all legacy DOUBLE values and refuses to discard versioned invoices or capacity holds.

On invoice creation, the server locks the selected group, removes expired holds, checks capacity, and writes the invoice, its exact selected group details, immutable price snapshot, and hold in one database transaction. The invoice belongs to the authenticated user. A checkout key and pricing fingerprint suppress duplicate creation; reusing a key for a different selection returns 409. Payment initiation creates one fake transaction and authority per invoice. Its callback requires the matching fixture marker, version, exact total, selected group detail, and fake transaction amount before changing state. Success books capacity; failure releases it; expiry marks the invoice and transaction terminal and releases capacity. Duplicate or reversed callbacks do not change terminal states. A fixture-only scheduled sweep runs every minute when the isolation gate is satisfied; invoice creation also cleans expired holds for the selected group. Test-only fixture endpoints can advance a hold to expiry. The fixture hold is **15 minutes only**. No live hold duration has been approved.

## Decisions required before production checkout

1. Approve the base fare and optional service catalog, participant categories and age boundaries, quantities, price validity dates, and who may purchase for companions.
2. Approve the settlement currency and whether conversion is allowed. If so, specify the rate source, timestamp, precision, and rounding sequence.
3. Approve tax jurisdiction and exemptions, coupons and discount order, invoice legal content, and invoice expiry.
4. Approve capacity-consuming reservation states, hold duration, expiry scheduler, retry and cancellation rules, and treatment of legacy `group_users` bookings.
5. Select a gateway per currency and define its minor unit convention, signed callback verification, reconciliation, refund, and failure contracts.
6. Approve the invoice and reservation lifecycle for production, including notification and downstream booking integration behavior.

Live checkout requires approved and versioned commercial rules, provider credentials and callback verification, migration and reconciliation of legacy payment authority data, production-safe capacity cleanup, integration tests against provider sandboxes, and an explicit code path that replaces the current HTTP 409 gate. None of those activation conditions are met by the synthetic fixture.

### Pre-merge legacy-data audit

The original in-place `DOUBLE` to `DECIMAL(12,2)` migration silently changed legacy `125.125` to `125.13`, `0.005` to `0.01`, and `123456789.98765` to `123456789.99`; rollback could not restore those values. It also failed on `10000000000.01` as out of range. The corrected migration ran against a separate disposable MySQL 8 database populated with legacy group price items, paid and pending invoices, details, and a commercial transaction, including fractional and out-of-range amounts. All original monetary values and states had identical before and after checksums. Rollback restored the original DOUBLE column types and preserved the same checksum. The migration also succeeded with duplicate historical invoice IDs, since it no longer imposes a new uniqueness constraint on that legacy column. A rollback with a versioned invoice was correctly refused before dropping data.

Production-mode route inventory contains zero synthetic endpoints, and direct requests to all six endpoint shapes returned 404. The fake callback is tested with a commercial invoice, a forged fake transaction and hold, and a payment facade mock that forbids provider calls; it leaves all three records unchanged. The older `/payment/verify` route now rejects fake transactions and synthetic invoices before reaching its payment facade. The fixture suite also verifies callback idempotency, terminal-state protection, expired holds, and simultaneous capacity reservations.
